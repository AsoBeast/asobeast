One definition per term, in the wording the rest of this site uses.

## ASO

App Store Optimization: the practice of improving how an app listing ranks and converts in store search and charts. asobeast measures it from public store data.

## Storefront

One country's version of a store, identified by a two lowercase letter code such as `us`, `gb` or `de`. Catalogs, search results and rankings differ between storefronts.

## Home storefront

The single storefront recorded on an app row, taken from the imported URL or from `DEFAULT_COUNTRY`. It drives metadata snapshots, category ranks and reviews. See [Countries and markets](/concepts/countries).

## Market

A storefront you track keywords in. An app has one home storefront but can track keywords in many markets.

## Tracked keyword

The link between one app and one keyword row, carrying the source it came from and an optional relevance override. See [Track keywords](/guides/track-keywords).

## Keyword field

The private 100 byte App Store field that never appears on the public listing. It cannot be collected, so you paste it manually. Google Play has no equivalent. See [App Store and Google Play](/concepts/stores).

## Subtitle

The 30 character indexed App Store field below the title. App Store only. Its audit weight is 0 on Google Play.

## Short description

The 80 character indexed Google Play field, called `summary` in the store payload. Google Play only, and asobeast tracks its keywords automatically with the source `DESCRIPTION`.

## Position

An app's 1 based rank for one keyword on one UTC date. `null` means checked and not found within that row's depth. See [Positions and rank depth](/concepts/positions).

## Depth

How far a position check looked, stored on each observation. The default is 200, which is why an unranked keyword renders as `>200`.

## SERP

The search results page for one keyword. asobeast stores the top 10 results per keyword and date, which is what powers volatility, entrants and movers.

## Volatility

A 0 to 100 measure of how much a keyword's top 10 changes membership, averaged across consecutive observed days over an eight day window. Reordering alone scores 0.

## Mover

An app whose position on a tracked keyword changed materially between SERP snapshots.

## Entrant

An app that appeared in a keyword's top 10 without having been there before. Entrants fire the `serp.entrant` alert.

## Popularity score

A persisted 0 to 10 estimate of a keyword's search demand, shown on a 0 to 100 scale as volume, stored with its evidence source, formula version, capture date and confidence. See [Popularity, difficulty and opportunity](/concepts/scoring).

## Difficulty

A persisted 0 to 10 estimate of how hard a keyword's current top results are to displace, shown on a 0 to 100 scale. See [Popularity, difficulty and opportunity](/concepts/scoring).

## Opportunity

A 0 to 100 score for one keyword: search volume on a log scale multiplied by a difficulty gate, so either one can veto the keyword. It is computed on read and never stored. See [Popularity, difficulty and opportunity](/concepts/scoring).

## Suggest reach

How early a store offers the exact keyword while it is being typed: after how many characters and in which position, only for the whole phrase, or never. It is the main demand signal behind Google Play popularity; App Store popularity reads the search results instead. See [Popularity, difficulty and opportunity](/concepts/scoring#how-is-popularity-estimated).

## Score signals

The evidence returned with each stored score: suggest reach, how much of the first page targets the keyword, page flags such as `brand` or `padded`, and Apple's own popularity when it was used. See [Popularity, difficulty and opportunity](/concepts/scoring#what-are-score-signals).

## Visibility

A single number rolling up an app's positions across its tracked keywords. Compare it with itself over time rather than between apps.

## Category rank

An app's position in a store chart rather than in search. asobeast captures free, paid and grossing charts daily for the home storefront. See [Follow category ranks](/guides/category-ranks).

## Snapshot

A captured copy of a listing's metadata at one moment, stored with the raw store payload so a corrected parser can reprocess it.

## Change event

A field level difference between two consecutive snapshots, recorded for your own apps and for competitors. See [Watch listing changes](/guides/listing-changes).

## Change impact

How the positions of your tracked keywords and your visibility moved 7, 14 and 28 days after a change to your own listing, compared with the last rank check before it. See [Watch listing changes](/guides/listing-changes#measure-the-impact-of-a-change).

## Audit score

The weighted result of the ten factor audit rubric, out of 100. Weights differ per store. See [Run an ASO audit](/guides/aso-audit).

## Action item

One recommendation from the Action Center, carrying a rule, a priority, an estimated impact, its evidence and a lifecycle state. See [Work the Action Center](/guides/action-center).

## Fingerprint

The stable identity of an action, derived from its rule, app, store, country, keyword and discriminator. It excludes every volatile magnitude, which is what lets a snooze or a dismissal survive the next run.

## Linked app group

A group holding at most one app per store, used to compare the App Store and Google Play listings of the same product. See [Compare a portfolio](/guides/portfolio).

## Portfolio

Every tracked app in the workspace, rolled up into shared totals and trends.

## Workspace

The tenant boundary on every owned row. asobeast seeds one default workspace and every account shares it, so multi tenancy is prepared rather than implemented.

## Owner

The first account created on a fresh instance. Only the owner reaches the queue dashboard and the OpenAPI surface. See [The queue dashboard and the OpenAPI surface](/security/admin-surfaces).

## Personal API token

A revocable credential prefixed with `asob_`, minted in Settings and sent as a bearer header. Stored as a SHA-256 hash, shown once. See [Personal API tokens](/security/api-tokens).

## Daily pipeline

The scheduled run that enqueues position checks, snapshots, reviews and category ranks, then generates actions and flushes batched alerts. It starts at `CRON_DAILY`, defaulting to 03:00 UTC.

## Request budget

The estimated daily store request fan out against the capacity set by `SCRAPE_ITUNES_RPM` and `SCRAPE_GPLAY_RPM`. `GET /jobs/budget` returns it. See [The daily pipeline and rate limits](/concepts/pipeline).
