The changes feed is the answer to "what did they change, and when". It records field level differences between consecutive snapshots for your own apps and for every competitor you track. For your own changes, the card above the feed also shows how your positions moved afterwards.

## Read the changes feed

Each event names the app, the field, the previous value, the new value and the date it was detected.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| App | Yours or a competitor's |
| Field | The metadata field that changed |
| From and to | The two values, so you can read the edit itself |
| Detected | The UTC date the change was observed |

Detection is snapshot based, so the date is when asobeast noticed rather than when the store published. A refresh you trigger yourself narrows that gap. See [Import an app](/guides/import-an-app).

## Measure the impact of a change

A change to your own listing is only worth repeating if it worked. The card at the top of the changes page answers that for each change: how the positions of your tracked keywords and your visibility moved 7, 14 and 28 days later.

### What counts as one change

Every edit asobeast detects on your listing on one UTC day is one change, whatever the fields: a new title and a new subtitle found on the same day are measured together, and the card lists both. Version, price, screenshots, icon and What's New count too. On the App Store a new keyword field only takes effect with a new version, so a version change is where a keyword field edit lands.

Competitor changes are never measured here. They stay in the feed below the card.

### How a window is measured

Each change is compared with a baseline: the most recent rank check before the day of the change, at most 3 days before it. Each window then reads the most recent rank check between 2 days before its target date and the target date itself, where the target is the day of the change plus 7, 14 or 28 days.

| The window says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Measured on a date | both rank checks exist and at least one keyword was checked on both days |
| Measurable on a date | the target date has not arrived yet |
| No rank check near a date | no usable rank check was recorded in the 3 days up to the target |

A change with no rank check in the 3 days before it cannot be measured at all, and the card says so instead of showing its windows. That happens to an app imported just before its first change, or after a pause in the daily pipeline.

### Which keywords count

A keyword counts in a window when it was checked on both days, whether or not it was found. A keyword you started tracking after the change has no baseline, so it drops out instead of appearing as a new ranking. Paused keywords and keywords of other markets never count.

### Read the numbers

| Number | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Improved, declined | keywords ranked on both days whose position went up or down |
| Started ranking | not found within the checked depth before, ranked after |
| Stopped ranking | ranked before, not found after |
| Checked on both days | every keyword the window measured, including those found on neither day |
| Median position | the middle position change over keywords ranked on both days; up means the typical keyword climbed |
| Visibility | before and after, over the measured keywords, each weighted by its popularity on the baseline date for both numbers |

Holding the popularity weights at the baseline means a weekly rescore between the two dates cannot move the visibility change; only positions do. It is also why the number can differ slightly from the overview chart, which weights each day by the popularity current on that day and covers every active keyword in every market.

### When changes overlap

A window lists every later change to your listing that falls inside it, up to the day it was measured. Its effect is then mixed with that later change. Nothing is hidden or discounted: read the two changes together, or wait for a change with a clean window.

### Markets and windows

The report measures one market at a time. It opens on your home storefront; pick another market in the card when the app tracks keywords in more than one. The changes themselves are the same in every market, because asobeast keeps one metadata snapshot per app, for its home storefront. See [Countries and markets](/concepts/countries).

The card follows the window of the changes page, 30, 90 or 365 days, and measures the 12 most recent changes in it. When there are more, it says how many it measured out of how many. Rank checks older than `RETENTION_RANKINGS_DAYS` (365 days by default) are pruned, so a change older than that cannot be measured.

<Warning>
The report shows what moved after a change, not what the change caused. Store algorithm updates, seasonality, competitors' own edits and ordinary movement from one day to the next all shift positions too. Treat a window as evidence to weigh, and compare changes made under similar conditions.
</Warning>

The same report is available from the API as `GET /apps/{id}/changes/impact` with optional `days` and `country` parameters (see [asobeast API](/api-reference/introduction)), and to an agent as the `change_impact` tool (see [MCP tool reference](/mcp/tools)).

## Why competitor changes matter most

Your own changes you already know about. A competitor rewriting a title is the interesting row, because it usually precedes a position shift on the keywords that title covers.

Pair a competitor change with a position drop on the same keywords and you have the beginning of an explanation. The Action Center formalises this in `rank.investigate_drop`, which looks for an indexed field change within 14 days of a visibility fall. See [Work the Action Center](/guides/action-center).

## Get notified

Subscribe a channel to `metadata.changed`. In the default batched delivery mode, owned app changes and competitor changes arrive as two separate reports, so a channel can subscribe to one scope without the other. See [Send alerts](/guides/alerts).

## How long are changes kept?

`RETENTION_CHANGE_EVENTS_DAYS` defaults to `0`, which keeps change events forever. See [Configuration reference](/configuration/reference). That default is deliberate: change history is small, and it is the record you most want years later when explaining a ranking trend.

## Troubleshooting

- **A change you made is not listed.** No snapshot has been captured since the store published it. Refresh the listing.
- **A change appears with no visible difference.** Stores sometimes reformat whitespace or punctuation. The diff shows exactly what differed.
- **Competitor changes stopped.** Confirm the competitor is still tracked and that its listing has not been removed from the storefront.
- **Every window says Measurable on a date.** The change is recent. The first window arrives a week after it.
- **A window says No rank check near a date.** The daily pipeline did not check your keywords around that day. See [The daily pipeline and rate limits](/concepts/pipeline).
- **A change is missing from the impact card.** It is older than the page window, or at least 12 more recent changes came after it.
