The audit turns a listing into a score out of 100 with a letter grade, shows how much of that score was actually measured, and ranks the fixes by the points they would add.

## Run an audit

Open the audit tab on an app. The audit runs on every visit and needs no button: every check is a rule over stored data, so the same listing and the same data always produce the same score. A change in the number means a change in the listing, its rankings or its reviews.

The optional AI only reads images. It reports what is visibly on the icon and the screenshots, such as a caption, the language of that caption or whether the icon carries text, and the rules score what it read. It never gives a score of its own. See [Enable the optional AI features](/guides/ai-features).

## Read the score

Each factor carries a store specific weight. Google Play has no subtitle or keyword field, so those factors are not listed there, and the short description takes their place.

| Factor | App Store weight | Google Play weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | 20 | 20 |
| Subtitle | 15 | not listed |
| Keyword field | 15 | not listed |
| Short description | not listed | 15 |
| Description | 5 | 15 |
| Screenshots | 15 | 15 |
| Preview video | 5 | 5 |
| Ratings and reviews | 15 | 15 |
| Icon | 5 | 5 |
| Keyword rankings | 10 | 10 |
| Freshness and trust | 5 | 5 |
| **Total** | **110** | **105** |

The description weighs 15 on Google Play because Google Play indexes it. On the App Store, preview videos are not visible in public store data, so that factor is reported as not measurable rather than scored.

Every factor is a group of checks scored 0 to 10. A check that cannot be answered yet, for example because it needs competitors or the creative analysis, is listed as unanswered with what would answer it. The share of a factor's checks that are answered is its confidence, and the overall score weights each factor by its weight times its confidence. A factor measured by one light check therefore moves the overall far less than a factor measured completely, and missing data neither inflates nor lowers the score.

The overall confidence is the share of the rubric that was measured. The page shows it next to the score.

| Grade | Overall | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 85 to 100 | Excellent |
| B | 70 to below 85 | Good |
| C | 50 to below 70 | Fair |
| D | 30 to below 50 | Weak |
| F | below 30 | Poor |

A grade is called provisional while less than 60% of the rubric is measured. The factors are also split into two groups, search visibility and conversion, each with its own score.

## Checks and what they read

Each check reads one kind of data: the store listing, your tracked keywords, your competitors, your reviews, your rankings or the AI creative analysis. The exact thresholds live in [the check sources on GitHub](https://github.com/AsoBeast/asobeast/tree/main/apps/api/src/audit/checks), so this page names the checks without repeating numbers that could drift from the code.

### Search visibility

| Check | Store | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title keyword, length, policy and uniqueness | Both | Keywords, listing, competitors |
| Subtitle keyword, repetition and length | App Store | Keywords, listing |
| Keyword field bytes, hygiene and relevance | App Store | Your saved keyword field |
| Short description keyword, length, repetition and policy | Google Play | Keywords, listing |
| Description keyword coverage, frequency and placement above the fold | Google Play | Keywords, listing |
| Search visibility, top 10 share and weekly trend | Both | Rankings |
| Keyword gap to competitors | Both | Competitors, rankings |

### Conversion

| Check | Store | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Description hook, call to action, social proof and formatting | Both | Listing |
| Screenshot count | Both | Listing |
| iPad screenshots | App Store, iPad apps | Listing |
| Feature graphic | Google Play | Listing |
| Screenshot captions, first message, style consistency | Both | AI creative analysis |
| Keywords in screenshot captions, caption localization | App Store | AI creative analysis, keywords |
| Promo video linked | Google Play | Listing |
| Icon text, simplicity, contrast and distinctness | Both | AI creative analysis, competitors |
| Average rating and rating volume | Both | Listing, competitors |
| Recent review sentiment | Both | Reviews |
| Current version rating | App Store | Listing |
| Replies to negative reviews | Google Play | Reviews |
| Update recency and release notes | Both | Listing |
| Localizations | App Store | Listing |
| Privacy policy linked | Google Play | Listing |

Caption localization compares captions with the storefront's language only when your app declares that language, so an English only app in a non English storefront is not penalised.

## Follow the plan

Every check that warns or fails carries a title, the evidence behind it and a fix. The plan ranks them in three buckets.

| Bucket | Horizon | What lands there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick wins | Today | Text edits you make in the store console in minutes |
| High impact | This week | Creative work, such as screenshots or the icon, that adds at least one point |
| Strategic | This month | Work that needs releases, time or users, and creative work that adds less than a point |

- **Lift** is how many points the overall score would gain if that one check reached 10.
- **Impact** is high from 3 points of lift, medium from 1 and low below that.
- **Effort** is minutes, hours or weeks.
- **Potential** is the overall score with every advised check fixed.

Each recommendation links to where the fix happens: the metadata workbench, the keywords page, the rankings, the competitors or the reviews. Fixes made in App Store Connect or Play Console, such as new screenshots, carry no link.

## Unlock more of the audit

An unanswered check says what would answer it. The page lists these unlocks ranked by how much of the rubric they would measure.

| Unlock | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Keyword field | Paste your keyword field from App Store Connect on the keywords page |
| Keywords | Score your tracked keywords so the audit knows your primary keywords |
| Competitors | Add competitors to compare titles, icons, ratings and keyword gaps |
| History | Wait for the daily checks; the weekly trend needs 7 days of positions |
| Reviews | Recent review checks need 5 reviews from the last 30 days |
| AI analysis | Run the creative analysis to read the icon and screenshots |

## What the audit cannot see

Public store data leaves some things out. The audit lists them on every run instead of guessing.

### App Store

| Limitation | Why |
| --- | --- |
| App previews | asobeast cannot see App Store previews. Check them in App Store Connect. |
| Promotional text | Not in the data asobeast reads, and not indexed. |
| In-app events | Not visible to asobeast, although events can appear in search. |
| Custom product pages | Not visible to asobeast, although pages with assigned keywords appear in organic search. |
| Replies to reviews | The App Store review feed carries no developer replies. |
| Rating prompts | Happen inside your app. |
| Product page conversion | App Store Connect analytics only. |

### Google Play

| Limitation | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Promo video content | asobeast sees whether a video is linked, not what it shows. |
| Promotional content | Offers and events published from Play Console are not visible. |
| Custom store listings | Not visible to asobeast. |
| Store listing experiments | Run and read in Play Console. |
| Android vitals | Crash and ANR rates affect visibility but are Play Console only. |
| Rating prompts | Happen inside your app through the In-App Review API. |
| Store listing conversion | Play Console only. |

## Follow the history

A score snapshot is written daily on `CRON_AUDIT`, which defaults to `0 6 * * *`, meaning 06:00 UTC, after the daily pipeline. A finished creative analysis also records today's point, so the chart moves as soon as the checks it answers are scored.

Snapshots recorded before this rubric are kept, and the chart marks the day the rubric changed with a "Rubric v2" line so a jump on that day is not mistaken for a change in the listing.

Audit score rows follow `RETENTION_AUDIT_SCORES_DAYS`, which defaults to `0` and therefore keeps them forever.

## Troubleshooting

- **The score changed after upgrading.** The rubric changed to v2: new checks, confidence weighting and store specific factors. Compare points after the "Rubric v2" marker with each other, not with the points before it.
- **A factor says not measured.** None of its checks can be answered yet. Follow the unlock it shows, or read the limitation if public data cannot measure it.
- **The analysis is outdated.** Your icon, screenshots, competitor icons or `AI_MODEL` changed since the last analysis. Its checks stay unanswered until you analyze again.
- **The analysis failed.** The page shows the reason. See the error table in [Enable the optional AI features](/guides/ai-features#troubleshooting).
- **The score differs between an iOS and an Android listing of the same product.** Expected. The factors and weights differ by store.
