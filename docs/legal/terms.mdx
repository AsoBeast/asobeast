## A self hosted installation

A copy you run on your own server is governed by [AGPL-3.0-only](https://github.com/AsoBeast/asobeast/blob/main/LICENSE) alone. The terms do not apply to it, because there is no service between you and your own deployment.

## What the terms rely on

The terms state their promises in plain words and leave the exact figures to these pages:

- [Pricing](/legal/pricing) lists every plan limit and rate limit. The terms make those limits part of the agreement.
- [Rate limits](/api-reference/rate-limits) describes the headers and the `429`, `403` and `402` answers.
- [Data export, deletion and retention](/legal/data-retention) describes what deletion reaches and when.
- [How asobeast collects data](/legal/data-collection) sets out the dependence on Apple and Google in full.

## The hosted service in numbers

The terms promise best effort availability from a single server. This is what that means:

| Promise | Value | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Availability target | None | Best effort. We publish what happens rather than a percentage we have not earned |
| Planned downtime | Minutes, announced | Upgrades stop the stack, apply migrations and start it again |
| Recovery point objective | Up to 24 hours | One backup a day. Losing the machine loses at most a day of collection, and never your account or your app list, which change rarely |
| Recovery time objective from the local archive | Minutes | Restoring on the same machine is a database restore and a restart |
| Recovery time objective from the offsite archive | Hours | Rebuilding on another machine is bounded by transferring the archive, not by the restore |
| Backup retention | 56 days | 14 daily and 8 weekly archives, encrypted, held both on the machine and offsite |

Incidents that affect collection are posted on a status page hosted away from the service at [status.asobeast.com](https://status.asobeast.com). See [Backups](/operations/backups) for how the archives are taken and restored.
