Nine prompts that produce useful answers, each with the tools it exercises. Naming the app, the market and the window is what separates a good answer from a vague one. The last two change things, so they need a token with the write scope.

## Run a weekly ASO review

```text
Give me a weekly ASO review. Start with the portfolio roll-up, then the action summary,
then visibility over the last 14 days for any app that lost ground. Tell me the single
thing to do first and why.
```

Exercises `portfolio`, `actions_summary` and `visibility_history`.

## Review last month's Action Center work

```text
Summarize my Action Center work over the last 30 days: how many actions opened, how many
I marked done, how many resolved on their own and how many were confirmed fixed. Then list
the actions that are done, open each one and tell me which outcomes improved, which
worsened and which are still being measured, with the before and after values.
```

Exercises `actions_activity`, `list_actions` and `get_action`.

An outcome is a measured change in the rule's metric between the day you closed the action and the latest check, not proof that your change caused it. See [Work the Action Center](/guides/action-center#outcomes).

## Triage this week's keyword losses

```text
For app <id>, list the tracked keywords in the us storefront whose position fell over the
last 7 days. For each, say whether the SERP itself shifted or only my position did.
```

Exercises `list_keywords`, `ranking_history` and `serp_movers`.

## Explain why a keyword moved

```text
Keyword <id> dropped this week. Show its position history for the last 30 days, the most
recent SERP snapshot, and any change events on the app in that window. Then give me the
two most likely explanations.
```

Exercises `ranking_history`, `serp_snapshot` and `changes_timeline`.

## Compare against a competitor in one market

```text
Compare app <id> with the top ranked competitor on keyword <id> in Germany. Use the SERP
snapshot for who is ahead, and visibility history for the trend over 30 days.
```

Exercises `app_summary`, `serp_snapshot` and `visibility_history`.

## Draft the next metadata iteration

```text
For app <id>, show the metadata audit and the latest ASO audit, then pull keyword
suggestions from the competitors strategy. Propose a title and subtitle that stay inside
the character limits and cover the highest opportunity uncovered keywords.
```

Exercises `metadata_audit`, `app_audit` and `keyword_suggestions`.

The server cannot apply a draft, and neither can asobeast. Every metadata change is published by you in App Store Connect or Google Play Console. See [Optimize listing metadata](/guides/metadata-workbench).

## Track the best suggestions in one market

```text
For app <id>, pull keyword suggestions from the competitors strategy, drop the ones I
already track in the de storefront, and show me the five with the best opportunity. Wait
for my approval, then track them in de.
```

Exercises `keyword_suggestions`, `list_keywords` and, with a write token, `track_keywords`.

Each phrase that is new to your instance queues a store scoring job and joins the daily ranking run, so add the few that matter. Tracking counts against your plan's keyword market limit. See [Countries and markets](/concepts/countries).

## Close the actions you have already handled

```text
List the open actions for app <id>. For each one that my last release already covers, say
why and wait for my yes. Then mark those done with a note naming the release.
```

Exercises `app_actions`, `get_action` and, with a write token, `set_action_status`.

Marking an action done starts the measurement of its outcome, and the change appears in the history under the name of the person who minted the token. See [Work the Action Center](/guides/action-center#outcomes).

## Writing better prompts

Three habits do most of the work.

- Name the app and the market. Keywords are per storefront, so an unqualified question makes the agent guess.
- Give a window. `last 30 days` maps directly onto the `from` and `to` parameters, which must be real dates in order. See [API conventions](/api-reference/conventions#dates-are-utc).
- Ask for the reasoning. The tools return evidence, so asking the agent to show its arithmetic makes a wrong answer visible rather than plausible.
