# Keyword discovery rules

### Domain rule 26: Keyword discovery lists what an app ranks for and does not track, and it never scores, never writes a position history and never spends keyword room

`KeywordDiscovery` (tenant owned, RLS, `@@id([appId, keywordId])`) holds one proposed phrase per app: `source` is text validated in code against `DISCOVERY_SOURCES` (`keywordSourceOf` in `keyword-discovery/discovery-pool.ts` maps it to the `KeywordSource` relevance and tracking use), `depth` and `checkedOn` are nullable and null means never checked, `queuedAt` is the weekly reservation, `seenAt` is stamped every time the pool proposes the phrase and drives the 60 day retention (`KEYWORD_DISCOVERY_DAYS`), and `dismissedAt` is permanent: retention never prunes a dismissed row, because the row is what keeps the phrase out of the pool. The pool is `buildPool` over `DiscoveryCandidates.forMarket`: the listing of each market, the competitors' home titles and subtitles and the review phrases of two words or more (`MIN_REVIEW_PHRASE_WORDS`, so a single common word never takes a weekly search) for the home market only, Apple's popularity weeks of the app's genre for the App Store only, and the phrases the workspace tracks for other apps, minus every phrase the app tracks in that market (active or paused) and every dismissed one, cut to `DISCOVERY_POOL_LIMIT` per market; `DiscoveryPoolStore` writes it, keeps a checked row's relevance with its ranking evidence and never re-proposes or un-dismisses a dismissed phrase. A position is read for free from the `SerpEntry` capture of today or yesterday (`DiscoveryCaptures`, `depth` 50; a capture without the app is not final and the row stays due for its own search) or from one deferred `DISCOVER_KEYWORD` search to `RANK_DEPTH`, at most `DISCOVERY_CHECKS_PER_RUN` per app per ISO week counted by `queuedAt` and reserved under a per app advisory lock (`DISCOVERY_LOCK`); the cap is a constant, not a plan limit, because it protects the host address and self hosted has no plan limits, and a row is due again after `DISCOVERY_RECHECK_DAYS`. `KeywordDiscoveryPlanner.plan` is the one entry for the weekly `CRON_DISCOVERY` sweep (`KeywordDiscoverySweep`, every primary app the store still lists in the workspaces `ActiveWorkspaces.forDailyRun` names, no spend) and for `POST /apps/{id}/keyword-discoveries/refresh`, which spends `spider` only when at least one check is queued and releases the reservation when the spend or the enqueue fails; a delisted app and a market in `unlistedMarkets` get no search, and only the markets `loadAppMarkets` names (home plus every market the app tracks a keyword in) are searched; a free capture that ranks a row is auto tracked by the planner as a check would be. `DiscoveryCheck` runs inside `StoreJobsHandler`, gated by `CollectionEligibility.forWorkspace` like a deep search probe, searches through `SerpCapture`, stores the capture only when `CrossTenantAccess` counts no active tracker of the keyword, and never writes a `KeywordRanking` or a `KeywordMetric`. Tracking goes through `DiscoveryTracker.trackRows`: `planSeed` per country against the plan's keyword room, one `QuotaService.admitKeywordMarkets`, a `KeywordRanking` row for an observation of today or yesterday so the table never shows Checking for a known position, `FirstPositionChecks` for the rest, `enqueueFirstScores` and `MarketListingRequests`; a row over the room stays a discovery (`skipped`), and a discovery is hidden while the app tracks its keyword (`keyword: { tracked: { none: { appId } } }`), so untracking returns it. `App.autoTrackRanked` lives on `App`, not `AppSetup`, and a check that finds the app tracks the phrase while it is on, keeping it a discovery when the admission is refused. Opportunity is derived from a stored `KeywordMetric` (or Apple's listed popularity for an unscored App Store term), so Opportunities lists scored keywords only. `GET /jobs/budget` reports the weekly checks in an optional `discovery` block per store and in total, never in `total` or `utilization`, and the weekly job is not part of `AppRunStatusService`, the capacity report or the operator metrics. The MCP read tool `keyword_discoveries` wraps the list; no MCP write tool tracks a discovery.
