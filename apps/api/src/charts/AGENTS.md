# Top chart rules

### Domain rule 20: A chart is captured once a day for every workspace

`ChartCapture` and `ChartEntry` are shared store tables with no policy, like `Keyword`; each entry keeps the listing its storefront showed that day, because the feeds are localized per storefront. `ChartCaptureService.captureToday(key)` fetches only when today's capture is missing, reads an empty feed against the stored captures of the same chart (`admitEmpty`). When the newest earlier capture with entries holds at least `SPARSE_CHART_ENTRIES` (10) of them and is 1 to `IMPLAUSIBLE_LOOKBACK_DAYS` (7) days old the feed is implausible and throws the `StoreRequestError` on every attempt and on demand (502). Searches and review feeds follow the same rule without the entry threshold, treating any result or review in the lookback as a sign of a block, except that an App Store answer still empty on the last attempt of its job settles through `settleOnLastAttempt` (`store-providers/result-plausibility.ts`): a warning, nothing written and a completed job. An empty Google Play answer is a soft block and still fails at once. When the newest earlier capture is already empty it stores a capture with no entries at once. Otherwise a queued job stores the empty capture only on its last attempt (`isFinalAttempt`, passed from `StoreJobsHandler` through `checkCategory` as `CaptureOptions.finalAttempt`) and throws the `StoreRequestError` on the attempts before, so a soft block on a chart with no history is retried and never stored as an empty chart, and `POST /charts/capture`, which has no retries, stores nothing and answers 200 with `answeredNoApps: true` (`ChartListsNoAppsError`). A chart empty by nature (the Google Play paid Shopping chart) therefore succeeds, is asked once a day after its first day, and gives `checkCategory` `null` positions. `captureToday` never catches a store failure. The App Store call asks for `fullDetail: false`, and `CHART_DEPTH.APP_STORE` is 100 because the feed answers no more; Google Play is 200. `checkCategory` reads the capture and keeps writing tenant owned `CategoryRank` rows with that depth. The daily run captures every chart kind of each category and of the overall chart a tracked app sits in (`bucketsOf` iterates `CATEGORY_COLLECTIONS`), while `AppBucketRow.collections` stays the app's price class plus grossing and decides which rank rows are written, so a free app is never judged against the paid chart. `rankedChartsOf`, `isRankedIn` and `rankedChartKey` (`category-ranks/ranked-charts.ts`) are the one statement of that rule: `checkCategory` writes a rank row only for a chart the app is ranked in, and the `categories` stage of the run status expects exactly `CategoryRanksService.rankedCharts(appId)`, four charts for a free or paid app, so the stage can reach complete while the fan out still reads six. A `ChartFollow` is tenant owned, one row per chart kind, and the `chartFollows` quota counts the categories the rows belong to (`countChartCategories` in `QuotaService`, a distinct read on store, country, genre and device, `CHART_CATEGORY_COLUMNS`), so the three kinds of one category cost one unit and `admit` lets a further kind of a followed category through at the limit; `ChartFollowsService.buckets` schedules the oldest categories the plan has room for (`oldestCategories` in `charts/chart-category.ts`) with every kind each holds, deduplicated against the buckets of the workspace's apps in the daily fan out and in `GET /jobs/budget`, so a downgrade cannot keep more categories captured than it pays for. The web follows a category by posting one follow per missing kind and never asks for a new endpoint. `POST /charts/capture` runs under the store deadline and spends `chartCapture` only when a store request happens, so a chart already captured today and a refused key cost nothing. `captureToday` is single flight in process, keyed by the chart key and the UTC date like `ActionsAiService.explain`, so two first captures in one API process reach the store once and spend once; a caller that joined a flight whose leader was refused before the store (its own allowance spent) captures on its own allowance instead of inheriting the refusal, while a store failure is shared. Two processes still race and the unique index keeps one capture, which is the only cross process guarantee. The genre tables in `@asobeast/shared` are Apple's chart page and the Play scraper's enum, and a genre a DTO accepts is one of them or `overall`; the device is in the key with one value per store today. Movement and trending are pure functions of two captures (`charts/chart-movement.ts`): the comparison is the newest capture at or before the day minus the window and no older than one more window, and reading them never makes a store request. The page shows the three kinds of a category side by side, the top 25 of each (`CHART_COLUMN_ROWS`), and the table of one kind behind `?view=table`; `chartHref` links a category, `tableHref` links a kind. A capture with no entries is a page with `capture` set and `entries: []`; the web reads it with `listsNoApps` as **No apps in this chart**, offers no **Capture today** for a day that is already captured and never treats it as an uncaptured day. A capture answer with `answeredNoApps` is toasted and neither seeded nor stored.
